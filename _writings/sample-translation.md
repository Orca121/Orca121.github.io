This page is a **sample** of how a translation can sit here. Patrick Wong did not write it, and did not translate it.

The English is from William Wordsworth, “[I Wandered Lonely as a Cloud](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/I_Wandered_Lonely_as_a_Cloud)” (1807), which is in the public domain:

> I wandered lonely as a cloud  
> That floats on high o'er vales and hills,  
> When all at once I saw a crowd,  
> A host, of golden daffodils;

A placeholder rendering, only to show the form:

> 我独自漫步，像一朵云，  
> 高高飘过山谷与丘陵，  
> 忽然看见一大片，  
> 金色的水仙，成群攒聚。
